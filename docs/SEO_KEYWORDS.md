# SEO keyword map: AI CV Builder BD

Researched 2026-10-19 for https://aicvbuilderbd.online. Audience: Bangladeshi job seekers, mostly on Android
phones, searching in English, Bangla, and Banglish (Bangla written in Latin letters).

## How this was researched, and its limits

- I ran live web searches for English seeds ("cv format for job in bangladesh", "bdjobs cv format
  fresher", "free cv maker bangladesh online") and Bangla seeds ("সিভি ফরমেট চাকরির জন্য",
  "বায়োডাটা ফরমেট চাকরির"). The other seeds are mapped from the seed list and common spelling
  variants (সিভি/বায়োডাটা/জীবন বৃত্তান্ত; ফরমেট/ফরম্যাট both appear in ranking headlines).
  **Re-check them in Google Search Console after launch** (Performance → Queries, filtered by
  country = Bangladesh).
- **No search-volume numbers are given.** I had no keyword-volume tool. Where the map says "higher/lower
  demand", that is an **estimate** based on how many competing pages target the term and how often it
  appears in results. Treat it as a hypothesis to confirm with Search Console or Google Keyword Planner.

## What the search results show

- **Results are weak, thin or spammy.** For "cv format for job in bangladesh" and "bdjobs cv format
  fresher", the top results mix one real local publisher (Shomvob's CV-format guide and its PDF
  templates), university career pages (AIUB, BUTEX, a 2011 Daffodil forum post), and **auto-generated
  spam pages** (pythonanywhere/"norml" template farms). Useful, original, current content can compete.
- **Local norms the results confirm**, which our content and templates should reflect:
  - A short **career objective** of 2–3 sentences.
  - Education listed newest first, with **SSC/HSC/Honours/Masters and GPA/CGPA**.
  - **Personal details**: father's/mother's name, date of birth, religion, marital status, nationality,
    and NID, described as common but optional.
  - A passport-size photo, **references**, and a one-page CV for freshers.
- **Bangla results are owned by news publishers' career sections** (Jago News "jago-jobs", Jugantor
  job-seek, The Daily Star Bangla youth/career, Prothom Alo lifestyle) plus teachers.gov.bd blog
  posts. These are short opinion articles. None of them offers a tool, BD-format templates or FAQs, so a
  Bangla guide **with a working builder attached** has a clear angle. Their advice, which our content
  should agree with: use a simple layout and standard fonts, write a professional summary instead of
  a generic "hard-working" objective, describe experience with situation → action → result, make the
  CV ATS-aware, and avoid messy, patched-together information.
- **"বায়োডাটা ফরমেট" returns mostly irrelevant or spammy results** (Wikipedia, foreign PDF farms,
  a 2014 Jago News post). This looks like an under-served query, so it is a good early-win target.
- **Government, bank and university employers often prescribe their own format** (for example BUTEX
  posts its required CV format). Content should tell readers to check the circular first. That is helpful
  and builds trust.
- **Competitors:**
  - **bdjobs.com**: profile-based "My Bdjobs" CV, the dominant job board. We can't outrank the brand for
    navigational queries. Target "bdjobs **style/format** CV" informational intent, with a clear
    "not affiliated" note.
  - **Shomvob**: guides plus PDF templates. Template downloads, not a builder.
  - **Teamzlab BD CV Maker**: a free BD-format builder (SSC/HSC fields, photo, browser PDF). The closest
    direct competitor; it is a tool page with little guide content.
  - **Canva, Zety, Kickresume, Resume.io**: strong global builders, but English-only. They aren't BD-format
    aware (no SSC/HSC or personal details) and paywall downloads (Zety, Resume.io). **Our gaps to exploit:
    Bangla UI and Bangla CVs, BD sections, free PDF.**
- **"People also ask" style questions** collected from the results and the seed set, used for the FAQs:
  - What is the correct CV format for a job in Bangladesh?
  - How do I make a CV for my first job (fresher, no experience)?
  - Should I add my photo, NID, religion or father's name to my CV?
  - What is the difference between a CV, a resume and a biodata?
  - Which CV format is used for government jobs in Bangladesh?
  - How long should a CV be? Should it be one page?
  - How do I write a career objective?
  - How do I write a cover letter for a job in Bangladesh?
  - Can I write my CV in Bangla?
  - Is an ATS-friendly CV needed in Bangladesh?

## Search intent legend

- **T**: transactional/tool. The user wants to make a CV now, so send them to the builder.
- **I**: informational/how-to. The user wants a guide, so answer first, then the CTA.
- **C**: commercial investigation. The user is comparing templates or tools.

## Content rules

1. **Help first.** Each guide answers the query in the first screen. A short answer box comes first,
   then a structured walkthrough, a worked example, then FAQs.
2. **No keyword stuffing.** Use the primary keyword in the title, H1, first paragraph and URL. Use
   secondary keywords where they fit naturally in H2s and body text.
3. **Bangla pages are written for Bangla readers**, not machine-translated word for word. Keep common
   English job terms (CV, GPA, CGPA, ATS) because that is how people search.
4. **Internal links.** Every guide links to the builder, to 2–3 sibling guides and to templates. The
   home page links to every guide.
5. **Trust.** Don't make unverifiable claims (no "10K+ users" or "95% pass rate"). Say "not affiliated
   with bdjobs.com" on the bdjobs page. Tell readers to follow any format required by a circular.
6. **Freshness.** Guides show `dateModified`. Review them yearly, and whenever bdjobs or government
   circular formats change.
